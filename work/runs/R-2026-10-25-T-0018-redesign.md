## What is different now

Every list (Links, a tag, a project, Unsorted, Archive) now shows each link on two lines: the title on top, and under
it the source, the projects (dot and name) and the tags as plain text. Status and priority sit in a fixed spot on the
right, as a mark and a word, one above the other, so they line up down the whole list. A link's details show Status and
Priority as two side-by-side lists of four choices you can click or move through with the arrow keys; the title wraps
and becomes editable when clicked; the Organise, Archive and Delete buttons stay pinned at the bottom. Clicking a row's
time now opens it instead of archiving it. Day names sit above their card, selected rows get a thin blue bar, empty
pages say what to do, icons show on dark backgrounds, and only Unsorted keeps a count in the sidebar. On a phone there
is a bottom tab bar (Links, Unsorted with its count, Projects, Tags, More), More opens the old menu, and the details
rise as a sheet with a grab handle.

## Deviations from the plan

- **Phone breakpoint 759 px, not 720.** The plan says the tab bar is for "≤ 720 px"; the app's phone layout (rows,
  details sheet, organiser sheet) already switches at 759 px, so the tab bar follows that one edge rather than adding
  a second. Between 760 and 1099 px the top bar's Menu stays as before.
- **The `/` hint chip is drawn but does nothing yet**, as the plan's step 5 says ("a box with a `/` hint chip"); the
  key itself is T-0009's (shortcuts are out of scope). Until T-0009 lands it is a hint for a key that does not work.
- **The title is an auto-height textarea that looks like text until focused** (rather than text swapped for an input
  on click). Same behaviour for a person (wraps, a field on click, Enter or blur saves, Esc restores) and the older
  e2e (`getByLabel('Link title').fill`) needed no change.
- **The phone's one-line meta uses a fixed 18 px height**, not `1lh` (the plan's WebKit fallback, used from the start).
- **Unset status and priority** show the dashed circle and faint dash with no word (a screen reader hears "No status",
  "No priority"); the f3 shot showed a plain dash for both. The done_when names the dashed circle and dash.
- No lazy loading added: `/` is 53.83 kB, under the plan's 58 kB trigger.
