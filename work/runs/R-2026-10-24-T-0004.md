## What did not work

- `draw.mjs` deals at most two styles per kind, not three; three kinds were dealt instead (Deviations).
- Four of seven YouTube ids I tried were wrong (oEmbed 404) and are dropped; three real talks remain. Video length is
  not in oEmbed, so every link's "Length" shows a dash.
- The inspector measures a text node's glyph run, not its box, so any nowrap-ellipsis title whose full run happened to
  end near the screen edge was flagged; it took one round to see that. Titles now clamp by line.
- The row kebab is hidden on the phone (no hover there); the row menu is reachable from the opened link's sheet. The
  swipe the one-pager mentions is not built.
- C's light theme is the dark move inverted and is the least distinctive of the six pages.
- A page loads about 48 KB of data as plain JSON; fine for a mockup, not a bundle budget claim.
