# Plan: build ADR-0005, with two-line rows, radio-list status and priority, and the shared redesign

Built on ADR-0005 (OPT-0003: row f3, control c3, the whole shared redesign, chosen 2026-10-25).

## For the director (plain words)

**What this will do.** It builds the design you picked into the real app, on every list: Links, each tag, each
project, Unsorted and Archive.

- **Rows** become two lines. The title is on top; the source, projects and tags are underneath. Status and priority
  sit in a fixed spot on the right, as a mark and a word.
- **The link's details** get the two radio lists for status and priority, a title that wraps, quieter fields, and
  buttons pinned at the bottom.
- **Around the list:**
  - Day names move above their card.
  - Hover actions move after the time, so clicking the time can't archive anything.
  - Selected rows get a thin blue bar.
  - Empty pages say what to do in plain words.
  - Icons show on dark backgrounds.
  - Only Unsorted keeps a count in the sidebar.
- **On the phone:** a bottom tab bar, and the details open as a sheet.

One builder does it in its own branch, in this order: rows, then details, then the frame, then the phone. If time
runs out, what lands first is usable on its own. Two checkers then look at it: one at the tests, one at the screens
in Chrome and Safari's engine. Then you try it.

**Choices that are yours.** None. You made them in OPT-0003. The search box gets its new look but still searches
when you press Enter; search as you type is T-0009, next.

**Risk.**

- **Phone layout:** making whole items drop on the phone, instead of being cut mid-word, is the fiddliest part. If it
  can't be done cleanly in CSS, the builder stops and reports rather than shipping cut words.
- **Page size:** the first page load must stay under the 60 KB budget (52 KB today). If the redesign pushes past it,
  the builder moves the details and phone pieces to load on first use, as T-0008 did.

**Size.** About 5 hours of builder time, possibly over two sessions, then about an hour of checking. Nothing needs
setting up first.

## For the builder and verifier

### Approach

1. **Tokens and marks.**
   - Add `--icon-tile` (light: transparent; dark: #e4e5e8) and `--meta` (light #6b7079, dark #9a9ea6) to
     `design/tokens.css`.
   - Add `src/lib/ui/StateMark.svelte`, props `{ kind: 'status' | 'priority', value }`. It draws inline SVG:
     - status: an empty circle for To read, a half circle for In progress, a check in `--positive` for Done, and a
       dashed circle for null;
     - priority: 1 to 3 bars, three in `--negative`, and a faint dash for null.
   - Unit-test its render table. Afterwards one component draws every mark.
2. **Row (r3)** in `LinkList.svelte`.
   - **Grid columns:** favicon, body, a fixed 124 px state cell, the time, and an 80 px action slot.
   - **Body:** the title (one line at 1440, clamped to two at 390) over a meta line (`--meta`) of the source, projects
     (dot and name) and tags as text.
   - **Phone meta line:** a wrapping flex row clipped to one line height (`flex-wrap: wrap; height: 1lh;
     overflow: hidden`), so whole items drop instead of being cut.
   - **State cell:** status over priority, each a StateMark and a word.
   - Keep select mode, the paste flash, `hold()`, keyboard row focus, and the open link's fill.
   - Afterwards every list view shows the new row.
3. **Hover and select.**
   - Actions get their own slot after the time. The time cell is plain text, not covered by a button.
   - A selected row gets a 2 px `--accent` bar inside its left edge; the open row gets the active fill only.
   - Write an e2e test that clicks the time and expects the details open and the link not archived. It fails on
     main.
4. **Details (c3 and the shared panel)** in `Detail.svelte`.
   - Status and Priority become side-by-side `role="radiogroup"` lists: none plus the three, arrow keys moving within
     each group.
   - The title is text that turns into a field on click (wrapping, saves on blur or Enter, Esc cancels).
   - Source and Media become property rows: a 96 px label, then the value as a text-looking select, with "auto" as a
     small word.
   - Description and Notes are borderless on `--raised` until focused.
   - The footer is sticky to the panel's foot.
   - The organiser (`Picker.svelte`) values block uses the StateMark lists too.
5. **Frame.**
   - Day headings move out of the card onto the ground (13 px, `--muted`).
   - The search field gets the new look: a box with a `/` hint chip. The placeholder stays "Search" until T-0009
     makes it live.
   - Empty states, in the wording of `design/options/T-0016/fable/REPORT.md`, for: Unsorted empty, a tag or project
     with no links, and no results.
   - Sidebar: only Unsorted keeps its count.
   - Favicons sit on a `--icon-tile` square.
6. **Phone (≤ 720 px)** in `Shell.svelte`.
   - A fixed bottom tab bar: Links, Unsorted (with its count), Projects, Tags, More. It pads by
     `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, and the list pads for the bar.
   - More opens today's menu sheet (Archive, Settings, Log out).
   - The page's own h1 goes; the top bar names the screen.
   - The details sheet gets a grab handle.
7. **Size and lazy loading.** Run `npm run size`. If `/` goes over 58 KB, lazy-load Detail's new parts and the phone
   sheet.
8. **Tests and shots.** Add e2e for each `done_when`. Update older e2e only where a selector moved, never an
   assertion. Write Playwright shots to the run report's folder for QA.

### Choices made, and what lost

| Choice | Alternative | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| CSS-only "drop whole items" (wrap and clip to one line) | Measure in JS and hide overflowing items | No layout script per row; the spike showed it works. Stop and report if it fails in WebKit |
| One `StateMark` component with inline SVG | An icon font or CSS shapes | One source for every screen, themeable through tokens, tiny |
| A sticky footer inside the panel's scroll box | A fixed footer outside it | Keeps the panel one scroll area on desktop and in the phone sheet |
| Placeholder stays "Search" until T-0009 | "Filter as you type" now | The words would promise behaviour that doesn't exist yet |

### How it gets proved

| Done-when | Proved by |
|---|---|
| Rows, one status edge | e2e: collect the state cells' `getBoundingClientRect().left` on `/` at 1440; all equal |
| No mid-word cut at 390 | e2e: every visible meta item's right edge ≤ the row's content right edge |
| StateMark | unit render table (value → shape, token); QA visual |
| c3 radio lists | e2e: click and arrow-key changes save; reload keeps them |
| Details panel | e2e: footer buttons in the viewport at 1440×900 and 390×844 with a long description |
| Time click never archives | e2e that fails on main (run with `src` stashed and record it in the run report) |
| Frame items | e2e for the empty states and the sidebar counts; QA for day headings and the selected bar |
| Dark icon tile | QA in dark at 1440 and 390 |
| Phone tab bar and sheet | e2e at 390: each tab navigates; More shows Archive, Settings, Log out; the details sheet opens |
| Older flows | the whole existing e2e suite, unweakened (tests verifier diffs the test files) |
| QA, ladder, size | QA verifier in Chromium and WebKit at 390 and 1440, light and dark; `bonsai ladder --task T-0018` green at HEAD; the budget rung |

**Verifiers:** tests and correctness (opus), and QA (opus, Chromium and WebKit). Then the person looks.

### Risk in the code

- **WebKit and `1lh`:** support is recent. Fall back to a fixed `height` from `--lh-body`.
- **Sticky footer in the phone sheet:** it may fight the sheet's own transform. Test it at 390 in WebKit first.
- **Hover-slot change and older selectors:** this shifts the row's DOM, so older e2e selectors may break. Fix the
  selectors, never the assertions.
- **When to stop and report blocked:**
  - three red climbs on the same rung (the circuit breaker);
  - first load over 60 KB after lazy loading;
  - the drop-whole-items line can't be made to work in WebKit without a script.
